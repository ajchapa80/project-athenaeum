Final Validation Result

PASS

Lab 16 successfully implemented and validated a public, vendor-neutral alert-triage and decision-routing layer using deterministic, evidence-based rules.

The implementation preserved Lab 15 alert-record identity and validation context, created separate traceable triage-decision records, classified known/common, insufficient-data, unusual, and unknown conditions, and routed those decisions toward POLICY_EVALUATION or INVESTIGATION without treating triage as authorization or a final security conclusion.

Controlled validation confirmed that evidence quality takes priority over recognizable patterns, technical severity does not independently determine classification, uncertainty is preserved when appropriate, repeated processing produces consistent decisions with new TR identities, previous output remains intact, and no defensive action is authorized or executed.

Lab 16 satisfies the frozen controlled-validation requirements established before implementation.
