======================================================================
1. VALIDATION PURPOSE
======================================================================

The purpose of this validation was to confirm that the public-safe,
vendor-neutral Lab 17 policy-evaluation and approval-control layer behaves
exactly as defined in the frozen Requirements and Validation Plan created
before implementation.

Lab 17 begins from sanitized Lab 16-style triage records and adds:

- deterministic policy evaluation;
- supported-action checking;
- action-risk classification;
- explicit authorization outcomes;
- human-approval requirements;
- separate Policy Decision (PD) records;
- separate Approval (AP) records when approval is required;
- AR -> TR -> PD -> optional AP traceability;
- final workflow-state assignment;
- chronological policy / approval history;
- repeat-processing protection;
- batch summaries;
- fail-closed behavior.

Lab 17 does NOT execute remediation.

The validation specifically tested whether Business Guardian can determine:

    "Is this proposed response permitted by policy?"

and, when required:

    "Has explicit human approval been granted?"

without allowing severity, AI output, requested-action text, missing approval,
or unresolved investigation state to become authorization.
