======================================================================
1. PURPOSE
======================================================================

Lab 17 extends the validated Lab 16 alert-triage baseline with a public-safe,
vendor-neutral policy evaluation and approval-control layer.

Lab 16 answered:
    "What kind of condition is this, and where should it go next?"

Lab 17 answers:
    "Is a proposed response allowed by policy, does it require human approval,
     and what workflow state should the record enter next?"

Lab 17 does NOT perform remediation.

The lab must demonstrate that:
- a triage result cannot directly trigger a defensive action;
- technical severity cannot authorize an action;
- AI output cannot authorize an action;
- only supported, policy-defined actions may proceed;
- some low-risk actions may be pre-authorized;
- some actions require explicit human approval;
- denied, unsupported, prohibited, unusual, unknown, or insufficiently
  understood conditions do not bypass policy controls;
- approval decisions are recorded separately and remain auditable;
- approved actions are only marked READY_FOR_ACTION for a later execution
  layer;
- no defensive action is executed in Lab 17.

Lab 17 is a policy-and-authorization control layer, not an action engine.

2. DESIGN POSITION IN THE BUSINESS GUARDIAN LIFECYCLE
======================================================================

Current public-safe processing sequence:

    Security Source
        ->
    Ingestion / Normalization / Validation
        ->
    Alert Record (AR)
        ->
    Triage Decision (TR)
        ->
    Policy Evaluation
        ->
    Policy Decision (PD)
        ->
    Optional Approval Record (AP)
        ->
    Final Workflow State

Lab 17 final workflow states:

    READY_FOR_ACTION
    AWAITING_APPROVAL
    INVESTIGATION
    NO_ACTION_AUTHORIZED

Important:

READY_FOR_ACTION does NOT mean Lab 17 executes anything.

It means:
    "The proposed action has satisfied the policy/approval requirements
     demonstrated by this lab and may be handed to a future controlled
     action-execution layer."

The future execution layer must still perform its own:
- scope validation,
- target validation,
- permission checks,
- action-specific safety checks,
- execution,
- rollback handling where applicable,
- verification,
- audit logging.

5. IDENTIFIER AND TRACEABILITY MODEL
======================================================================

Existing identifiers MUST be preserved:

    AR-...  = Alert Record identity
    TR-...  = Triage Decision identity

Lab 17 introduces:

    PD-...  = Policy Decision identity
    AP-...  = Approval Record identity, only when approval is required

The relationship must remain:

    AR
     |
     v
    TR
     |
     v
    PD
     |
     +----> AP, when approval is required

Rules:

- A PD identifier must never replace an AR or TR identifier.
- An AP identifier must never replace AR, TR, or PD identifiers.
- Every PD must retain its source AR and TR identifiers.
- Every AP must retain its source AR, TR, and PD identifiers.
- Reprocessing the same source must create a new PD identifier.
- Reprocessing an approval-required case must create a new AP identifier.
- Existing output records must not be overwritten.

6. POLICY DECISION OUTCOMES
======================================================================

Lab 17 uses four frozen policy outcomes.

1. AUTHORIZED

Meaning:
The requested action is supported by the public demonstration policy and is
explicitly classified as low-risk and pre-authorized.

Result:
- approval_status = NOT_REQUIRED
- final_workflow_state = READY_FOR_ACTION

AUTHORIZED does NOT mean executed.

------------------------------------------------------------

2. REQUIRES_APPROVAL

Meaning:
The requested action is supported, but policy requires explicit human approval
before it may proceed.

Possible approval results:

PENDING:
- final_workflow_state = AWAITING_APPROVAL

APPROVED:
- final_workflow_state = READY_FOR_ACTION

DENIED:
- final_workflow_state = NO_ACTION_AUTHORIZED

------------------------------------------------------------

3. NOT_AUTHORIZED

Meaning:
Policy does not permit the requested action to proceed.

Examples:
- prohibited action;
- unsupported action;
- action outside the public demonstration catalog;
- policy fallback when no rule safely permits the request.

Result:
- approval_status = NOT_REQUIRED
- final_workflow_state = NO_ACTION_AUTHORIZED

------------------------------------------------------------

4. DEFERRED_TO_INVESTIGATION

Meaning:
The source triage record is not eligible for policy-based action evaluation
because the condition still requires investigation.

Examples:
- source next_stage = INVESTIGATION;
- source classification = UNKNOWN;
- source classification = UNUSUAL;
- source classification = INSUFFICIENT_DATA when routed to investigation.

Result:
- approval_status = NOT_REQUIRED
- final_workflow_state = INVESTIGATION

No requested action may override this gate.

======================================================================
7. APPROVAL STATUS VALUES
======================================================================

Frozen approval statuses:

    NOT_REQUIRED
    PENDING
    APPROVED
    DENIED

Definitions:

NOT_REQUIRED
Policy does not require a separate approval record for the current outcome.

PENDING
Human approval is required and has not been granted or denied.

APPROVED
A controlled explicit human approval response is recorded.

DENIED
A controlled explicit human denial response is recorded.

Safety rule:

Only APPROVED may satisfy an approval-required policy.

PENDING, absent approval, malformed approval, or any unrecognized value must
never be treated as approval.

======================================================================
8. FINAL WORKFLOW STATES
======================================================================

Frozen Lab 17 workflow states:

    READY_FOR_ACTION
    AWAITING_APPROVAL
    INVESTIGATION
    NO_ACTION_AUTHORIZED

READY_FOR_ACTION
The policy/approval requirements represented by Lab 17 are satisfied.
No action is executed.

AWAITING_APPROVAL
A supported action requires human approval and may not proceed yet.

INVESTIGATION
The source triage condition must return to or remain in the investigation lane.

NO_ACTION_AUTHORIZED
Policy or approval state prevents the proposed action from proceeding.

10. DETERMINISTIC POLICY RULE ORDER
======================================================================

Policy rules must be evaluated in this exact priority order.

RULE 1 - SOURCE / TRACEABILITY VALIDATION

Validate required identifiers, supported values, and structural fields before
policy evaluation.

A malformed or unusable policy input must fail safely.

No missing security-relevant value may be invented.

------------------------------------------------------------

RULE 2 - INVESTIGATION LANE GATE

If:
- next_stage = INVESTIGATION

Then:
- policy_outcome = DEFERRED_TO_INVESTIGATION
- approval_status = NOT_REQUIRED
- final_workflow_state = INVESTIGATION

This rule takes priority over the requested action.

A requested action must not pull an unresolved condition out of investigation.

------------------------------------------------------------

RULE 3 - UNSUPPORTED ACTION GATE

If requested_action is not in the public supported-action catalog:

- policy_outcome = NOT_AUTHORIZED
- approval_status = NOT_REQUIRED
- final_workflow_state = NO_ACTION_AUTHORIZED

No arbitrary action execution is allowed.

------------------------------------------------------------

RULE 4 - PROHIBITED HIGH-RISK DEMO ACTION

If:
- requested_action = DISABLE_USER_ACCOUNT

Then:
- policy_outcome = NOT_AUTHORIZED
- approval_status = NOT_REQUIRED
- final_workflow_state = NO_ACTION_AUTHORIZED

This public demonstration policy deliberately prohibits the action.

------------------------------------------------------------

RULE 5 - PRE-AUTHORIZED LOW-RISK ACTION

If:
- source next_stage = POLICY_EVALUATION
- requested_action = ADD_MONITORING_NOTE

Then:
- policy_outcome = AUTHORIZED
- approval_status = NOT_REQUIRED
- final_workflow_state = READY_FOR_ACTION

Technical severity does not create the authorization.
The explicit deterministic policy does.

------------------------------------------------------------

RULE 6 - APPROVAL-REQUIRED ACTION

If:
- source next_stage = POLICY_EVALUATION
- requested_action = TEMPORARY_BLOCK_SOURCE

Then:
- policy_outcome = REQUIRES_APPROVAL

Approval handling:

If controlled_approval_response = APPROVED:
    approval_status = APPROVED
    final_workflow_state = READY_FOR_ACTION

If controlled_approval_response = DENIED:
    approval_status = DENIED
    final_workflow_state = NO_ACTION_AUTHORIZED

If controlled_approval_response = PENDING or is absent:
    approval_status = PENDING
    final_workflow_state = AWAITING_APPROVAL

------------------------------------------------------------

RULE 7 - SAFE FALLBACK

If no earlier rule safely authorizes or routes the request:

- policy_outcome = NOT_AUTHORIZED
- approval_status = NOT_REQUIRED
- final_workflow_state = NO_ACTION_AUTHORIZED

Unknown policy behavior must fail closed.

======================================================================
15. SEVERITY SEPARATION REQUIREMENT
======================================================================

Technical severity remains separate from policy authorization.

Examples:

HIGH severity:
    does not automatically authorize blocking, disabling, isolation, or any
    other action.

LOW severity:
    does not automatically mean no approval is needed.

Policy behavior is determined by:
- source workflow lane;
- supported action;
- explicit action risk;
- deterministic policy rule;
- explicit human approval where required.

A severity field may be preserved for context and prioritization, but it may
not independently grant permission.

======================================================================
16. AI SAFETY REQUIREMENT
======================================================================

AI output may eventually:
- summarize evidence;
- explain conditions;
- recommend possible actions;
- help investigate;
- assist an analyst.

AI output may NOT:
- approve its own recommendation;
- bypass a deterministic policy rule;
- change a denied state to approved;
- convert alert/log text into executable instructions;
- invent an approval;
- invent missing authorization;
- create a supported action that is absent from the action catalog.

The policy engine is deterministic.

    unsupported means denied;
    unresolved means investigate;
    and no action occurs until a later controlled execution layer is allowed
    to act.

Lab 17 therefore prepares Business Guardian for future controlled remediation
without prematurely giving the system execution authority.
