======================================================================
1. PURPOSE AND OBJECTIVE
======================================================================

Lab Number:
18

Lab Title:
Controlled Adversary Simulation and End-to-End Detection Validation

Purpose:

The purpose of Lab 18 is to perform a controlled, authorized, end-to-end
validation of the Project Athenaeum / Business Guardian detection and
investigation workflow using owned, isolated virtual machines.

Objective:

Lab 18 must prove that a deliberately generated, bounded test event can be
observed across the complete read-only evidence path:

Kali
    -> Windows 11 workstation
    -> Wazuh
    -> Business Guardian read-only evidence connector
    -> investigation workflow
    -> sanitized validation evidence

The lab validates detection, evidence collection, traceability, investigation
workflow behavior, and safe documentation boundaries. It does not authorize
remediation, action execution, production use, or a determination that the
underlying condition is resolved.

These requirements are frozen before any Lab 18 VM activity begins.

======================================================================
2. ARCHITECTURAL POSITION AFTER LABS 15-17
======================================================================

Lab 15 established structured alert records, validation states, stable record
identity, processing history, and source traceability.

Lab 16 established deterministic alert triage and next-stage routing without
treating severity or classification as proof of malicious, benign, safe, or
resolved status.

Lab 17 established deterministic policy and approval boundaries without
executing defensive action.

Lab 18 validates the live evidence and investigation path that feeds and
supports those documented control concepts. It tests whether authorized lab
activity can be detected, collected read-only, investigated, traced, and
converted into sanitized evidence without crossing into remediation or action
execution.

Lab 18 does not replace or reimplement Labs 15-17. It demonstrates how live
Wazuh evidence and the private Business Guardian investigation capability fit
into the documented lifecycle.

The private Business Guardian read-only investigation vertical slice has
already been validated against a passing baseline of 264 tests. Lab 18 may
reference that validated baseline as readiness evidence. It must not expose,
copy, or reproduce proprietary implementation details from the private
repository.

======================================================================
3. END-TO-END VALIDATION PATH
======================================================================

The frozen validation path is:

1. Kali generates only an explicitly permitted controlled test condition.

2. The owned Windows 11 workstation receives or produces the expected local
   operating-system or security evidence.

3. The Wazuh agent and Wazuh server ingest, preserve, and expose relevant
   detection or event evidence.

4. The private Business Guardian connector performs read-only evidence
   collection from the authorized Wazuh source.

5. The Business Guardian investigation workflow creates or presents an
   investigation view that preserves source evidence, uncertainty, and
   traceability.

6. Selected evidence is manually reviewed and sanitized for Project
   Athenaeum documentation.

7. Public-safe Lab Notes, Screenshot Log entries, screenshots, and later
   validation results document what was observed without exposing private
   implementation details, credentials, sensitive configuration, or unsafe
   operational data.

Each stage must be validated independently. Evidence observed at a later stage
must not be used to claim that an earlier stage worked unless traceability back
to that earlier stage is preserved.

======================================================================
4. AUTHORIZED LAB BOUNDARIES
======================================================================

All Lab 18 activity is confined to owned and authorized isolated virtual machines under the operator's control.

Authorization applies only to the specifically identified Lab 18 VM
environment and its isolated lab network. It does not extend to public
internet hosts, third-party systems, employer systems, school systems,
neighbors, shared networks, cloud services, or any device not explicitly
owned and included in the lab.

Before controlled activity begins, the operator must verify:

- the intended VMs are running;
- the VMs are attached only to the expected isolated lab network;
- the Windows 11 target identity and IP address match the documented lab
  target;
- the Kali source identity and IP address match the documented lab source;
- the Wazuh server and agent relationship is healthy;
- no production or unrelated network route is in scope;
- the test activity and stop conditions are understood.

If the environment identity or network boundary cannot be confirmed, the test
must not begin.

======================================================================
5. SYSTEMS IN SCOPE
======================================================================

The following systems are in scope only when they are the owned, isolated Lab
18 instances:

- Kali Linux VM used as the controlled test source;
- BusinessGuardian-Win11-Workstation or the explicitly documented owned
  Windows 11 lab workstation VM;
- BusinessGuardian-Wazuh-Server or the explicitly documented owned Wazuh lab
  server VM;
- the Wazuh agent installed on the Windows 11 lab workstation;
- the authorized Wazuh dashboard, API, indexer, or evidence source needed for
  read-only validation;
- the private Business Guardian read-only evidence connector and investigation
  workflow used only against the authorized lab Wazuh source;
- the local Project Athenaeum Lab 18 documentation and AI artifact folders;
- manually selected sanitized evidence derived from the authorized test.

======================================================================
6. SYSTEMS AND NETWORKS OUT OF SCOPE
======================================================================

The following are out of scope:

- the public internet as a target;
- any third-party IP address, domain, endpoint, account, or service;
- the host operating system except for normal VM operation and evidence
  capture;
- home, guest, corporate, school, public, or shared networks;
- routers, access points, printers, phones, tablets, cameras, and other local
  devices not explicitly designated as Lab 18 VMs;
- cloud infrastructure or externally hosted services;
- production or customer Business Guardian environments;
- any Wazuh deployment other than the owned lab deployment;
- credentials, secrets, tokens, or private data unrelated to the controlled
  lab;
- either Git repository as a target of test activity;
- any system whose ownership, authorization, identity, or isolation is
  uncertain.

No discovery, probing, authentication activity, exploitation, or traffic may
be directed toward an out-of-scope system or network.

======================================================================
7. PERMITTED CONTROLLED TEST ACTIVITY
======================================================================

Permitted activity must be minimal, bounded, reversible where applicable, and
selected only to produce an expected observable event in the owned lab.

Permitted categories include:

- connectivity verification between the explicitly documented lab VMs;
- Wazuh agent-health and evidence-source verification;
- a small, rate-limited network discovery or connection attempt directed only
  at the owned Windows 11 lab workstation;
- a small, rate-limited set of controlled failed-authentication events using
  a lab-only account or otherwise approved lab mechanism;
- a benign controlled Windows event created specifically for detection and
  evidence validation;
- read-only inspection of Windows, Wazuh, and Business Guardian evidence;
- manual comparison of timestamps, identifiers, source/target details, rule
  information, event context, and investigation records;
- repeat execution of the same approved test condition when required to prove
  repeatability.

The exact test event must be selected and documented before execution. Only
one bounded test path should be introduced at a time so evidence can be traced
without ambiguity.

======================================================================
8. PROHIBITED ACTIVITY
======================================================================

Lab 18 prohibits:

- activity against any system not explicitly in scope;
- broad or uncontrolled scanning;
- denial-of-service or resource-exhaustion activity;
- malware, ransomware, destructive payloads, persistence, or propagation;
- credential theft, password spraying, brute force, or use of real personal
  credentials;
- exploitation intended to obtain unauthorized code execution or privilege;
- data destruction, encryption, corruption, or unauthorized modification;
- disabling security controls, logging, Wazuh services, or endpoint defenses;
- lateral movement outside the explicitly approved test path;
- evasion, anti-forensics, log deletion, or evidence tampering;
- copying private repository code or proprietary implementation detail into
  public Project Athenaeum artifacts;
- placing credentials, tokens, secrets, personal data, or sensitive network
  details in screenshots or public-safe evidence;
- executing a response or remediation action from Business Guardian;
- continuing after a stop condition is reached.

======================================================================
9. READ-ONLY BUSINESS GUARDIAN BOUNDARY
======================================================================

Business Guardian participation in Lab 18 is read-only.

The authorized private vertical slice may:

- connect to the authorized lab Wazuh evidence source;
- retrieve supported alert or event evidence;
- normalize or present evidence for investigation;
- preserve source references and timestamps;
- record collection and investigation workflow state;
- expose uncertainty, missing context, and validation warnings;
- prepare private investigation evidence for manual review and sanitization.

It may not:

- modify Wazuh evidence;
- modify the Windows 11 workstation;
- modify the Kali source;
- change firewall, account, service, endpoint, or network state;
- issue remediation commands;
- execute an approved or recommended action;
- claim a security condition is resolved;
- treat collected text or log content as executable instruction.

The previously validated 264-test baseline demonstrates the readiness of the
private read-only investigation vertical slice. Lab 18 validates its behavior
against authorized live lab evidence without publishing its proprietary
implementation.

======================================================================
10. NO REMEDIATION OR ACTION-EXECUTION BOUNDARY
======================================================================

Lab 18 ends at investigation and sanitized evidence validation.

No automated or manual remediation is part of the Lab 18 validation path.
No recommendation, classification, policy result, approval state, or severity
value grants execution authority in this lab.

The following actions are specifically outside Lab 18:

- blocking a source;
- changing a firewall rule;
- disabling or locking an account;
- isolating an endpoint;
- stopping or modifying a service;
- quarantining or deleting a file;
- changing system configuration;
- executing PowerShell or shell remediation;
- altering an alert or investigation record to indicate closure.

No condition is considered resolved in Lab 18.

======================================================================
11. EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS
======================================================================

Evidence must be sufficient to demonstrate the end-to-end path without
fabrication or unsupported interpretation.

Required evidence categories include:

- pre-test environment and isolation verification;
- source VM identity and controlled test initiation;
- target Windows 11 evidence showing the controlled condition or event;
- Wazuh agent-health evidence;
- Wazuh alert, event, dashboard, API, or index evidence associated with the
  controlled test;
- Wazuh rule, event, or source context when available;
- Business Guardian read-only collection result;
- Business Guardian investigation workflow state and evidence view;
- source-to-investigation traceability;
- sanitization review evidence;
- repeat-run evidence when repeatability is tested;
- any warning, missing evidence, mismatch, or failure observed.

Evidence must preserve the distinction between what the source reported, what
was collected, and what was interpreted.

COLLECTED evidence does not equal interpreted evidence.
COLLECTED evidence does not equal benign evidence.
COLLECTED evidence does not equal malicious evidence.
COLLECTED evidence does not equal resolved evidence.

======================================================================
12. TRACEABILITY REQUIREMENTS
======================================================================

A reviewer must be able to trace:

Controlled Test Activity
    -> Kali source identity and timestamp
    -> Windows 11 target identity and observed event
    -> Wazuh source evidence and detection timestamp
    -> Wazuh rule/event/source reference when available
    -> Business Guardian collection record or source reference
    -> Business Guardian investigation workflow evidence
    -> sanitized Project Athenaeum validation evidence

Traceability should preserve, when available and safe to document:

- test identifier;
- source VM and target VM roles;
- timestamps and time zone or UTC representation;
- source and destination addresses in sanitized or lab-safe form;
- Windows event identifiers and provider information;
- Wazuh alert, event, rule, agent, or source identifiers;
- Business Guardian collection and investigation references;
- evidence filenames;
- screenshot filenames;
- observed status at each stage;
- warnings, gaps, or failures.

Missing identifiers or context must be recorded as unavailable. They must not
be invented or silently substituted.

======================================================================
13. SANITIZATION AND PUBLICATION REQUIREMENTS
======================================================================

Before evidence enters public Project Athenaeum material, it must be reviewed
for:

- credentials, tokens, secrets, keys, cookies, and session information;
- personal names, usernames, email addresses, or unnecessary identifiers;
- host-system or unrelated device information;
- public IP addresses or unrelated network details;
- private repository paths, source code, internal class/function names, and
  proprietary implementation detail;
- sensitive Wazuh connection configuration;
- unnecessary raw logs or unrelated events;
- data that could expose or confuse the public/private boundary.

Public evidence must be the minimum needed to prove the validation result.
Sanitization must not alter the meaning of the observed outcome. Redactions or
omissions that affect interpretation must be documented.

Raw private evidence remains private. Only reviewed, intentionally sanitized
copies or representations may be used in public Project Athenaeum artifacts.

======================================================================
14. EXPECTED WAZUH DETECTION AND EVIDENCE CHECKPOINTS
======================================================================

The Wazuh validation path should establish the following checkpoints:

1. The Windows 11 agent is connected and healthy before the test.

2. The controlled event occurs on or against the intended Windows 11 VM.

3. Relevant endpoint or operating-system evidence exists for the event.

4. Wazuh ingests the relevant event or supporting evidence.

5. An expected Wazuh alert or event record is discoverable, or the absence of
   an expected detection is documented as a failure or investigation finding.

6. The Wazuh record preserves sufficient context to associate it with the
   controlled source, target, and test window.

7. Timestamps across Windows, Wazuh, and the controlled test are sufficiently
   aligned to support correlation.

8. The original Wazuh evidence remains unchanged by downstream collection.

A high severity, successful rule match, or visible alert is not proof that the
event is malicious, benign, authorized, remediated, or resolved.

======================================================================
15. BUSINESS GUARDIAN INVESTIGATION CHECKPOINTS
======================================================================

The Business Guardian validation path should establish:

1. The connector uses only the authorized read-only Wazuh evidence path.

2. Relevant Wazuh evidence is discovered or retrieved without modifying the
   source.

3. Collection status is recorded accurately.

4. Source identifiers, timestamps, agent information, rule/event context, and
   other supported evidence remain traceable.

5. Missing, malformed, ambiguous, or unsupported fields remain visible as
   warnings or unavailable evidence rather than guessed values.

6. Investigation workflow state is recorded without claiming remediation or
   closure.

7. Alert or log content is treated as untrusted evidence, not instruction.

8. The investigation view separates collected facts from analyst or system
   interpretation.

9. A COLLECTED status confirms evidence acquisition only. It does not assert
   that evidence has been interpreted, that activity is benign, or that a
   condition is resolved.

10. The resulting private evidence can be manually sanitized into a public-
    safe validation record without exposing proprietary implementation detail.

======================================================================

Lab 18 must capture enough evidence to support the validation path, but the

Required categories are:

======================================================================
17. PASS / FAIL ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
======================================================================

PASS requires all of the following:

- all activity remained inside the owned, authorized isolated VM environment;
- the selected controlled test was permitted and bounded;
- the Windows 11 target produced the expected event or observable evidence;
- Wazuh ingested relevant evidence;
- the expected Wazuh detection/evidence record was found, or an explicitly
  defined evidence-only outcome was satisfied;
- Business Guardian collected supported evidence through the read-only path;
- Business Guardian did not modify Wazuh, Windows, Kali, or network state;
- source-to-investigation traceability was preserved;
- COLLECTED was not treated as interpreted, benign, malicious, or resolved;
- no remediation or defensive action was executed;
- no condition was marked resolved;
- required evidence categories were documented;
- public evidence was sanitized without exposing proprietary detail;
- repeatability requirements passed when the repeat run was performed;
- no stop condition was violated;
- observed results were documented honestly, including warnings and gaps.

FAIL applies if any required condition above is not met, including:

- activity reached or may have reached an out-of-scope system;
- the VM/network boundary was not verified;
- expected Windows or Wazuh evidence was absent without a documented supported
  alternative;
- Business Guardian could not collect or trace the expected evidence;
- source evidence was modified;
- a remediation or execution boundary was crossed;
- collected evidence was misrepresented as interpreted or resolved;
- sensitive or proprietary information entered public evidence;
- evidence could not be traced reliably across the workflow;
- failures were hidden, overwritten, or reclassified to force a PASS.

A partial path is not an end-to-end PASS. Individual stages may pass while the
overall Lab 18 result remains FAIL or INCOMPLETE.

======================================================================
18. REPEATABILITY CRITERIA
======================================================================

After the first controlled run is documented and the environment remains safe,
the same approved test condition may be repeated.

Repeatability requires:

- the same authorized source and target roles;
- the same bounded test definition;
- the same expected evidence path;
- equivalent Windows and Wazuh evidence categories;
- equivalent Business Guardian collection and investigation behavior;
- preservation of original source evidence;
- preservation of first-run screenshots, notes, and validation evidence;
- new run timestamps and run-specific evidence references;
- no silent overwriting of prior outputs or records;
- documented explanation for expected nondeterministic values such as unique
  identifiers, ingestion timestamps, or record ordering;
- consistent safety and read-only boundaries across runs.

Repeatability does not require byte-identical live evidence. It requires the
same controlled condition to produce materially consistent and traceable
workflow outcomes.

======================================================================
19. STOP CONDITIONS
======================================================================

Testing must stop immediately if:

- any source, target, route, or system identity is uncertain;
- activity may be leaving the isolated lab network;
- an unexpected non-lab system responds;
- test volume or behavior exceeds the documented bound;
- VM, host, Wazuh, or network stability is degraded unexpectedly;
- the Wazuh agent or server becomes unhealthy in a way that could invalidate
  evidence;
- credentials, secrets, private data, or unrelated evidence appear in a
  planned public capture;
- Business Guardian attempts or appears able to modify source or endpoint
  state;
- any remediation or action-execution behavior is triggered;
- evidence integrity or timestamps cannot be trusted;
- the operator cannot distinguish the controlled event from unrelated
  activity;
- a prohibited activity occurs or is about to occur;
- authorization or ownership becomes unclear.

After a stop condition, preserve existing evidence, document what happened,
and do not resume until the cause is understood and the scope remains
authorized.

======================================================================
20. FAILURE-HANDLING EXPECTATIONS
======================================================================

Lab 18 failures must fail safely and remain visible.

Required behavior:

- stop the affected test path when safety or scope is uncertain;
- do not broaden activity to compensate for missing evidence;
- do not increase test intensity merely to force an alert;
- do not fabricate timestamps, identifiers, source context, or interpretations;
- record the stage at which the failure occurred;
- preserve available source evidence and prior-run evidence;
- distinguish no event, no ingestion, no detection, collection failure,
  investigation failure, traceability failure, and sanitization failure;
- keep warnings separate from confirmed failures;
- allow an individual stage to fail without misrepresenting other stages;
- document expected versus observed behavior;
- require human review before any retest;
- use a new run reference for a retest rather than overwriting the failed run.

Failure is an acceptable validation result when documented accurately. A false
PASS is not acceptable.

24. FINAL DESIGN STATEMENT
======================================================================

Lab 18 validates the controlled transition from live lab activity to Wazuh
detection, read-only Business Guardian evidence collection, investigation, and
sanitized documentation.

without converting detection into interpretation, collection into benign
status, investigation into remediation, or workflow progress into resolution.

Lab 18 stops before action execution.
