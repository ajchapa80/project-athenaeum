ARCHITECTURE BOUNDARIES

Security Source:
Where Business Guardian receives security information.

Managed Asset:
An administratively known workstation, server, device, or workload.

Asset correlation:
Explicit administrative context linking observed source identifiers to a
managed Asset. It is not technical identity proof.

Adding Asset context does not rewrite original security evidence.

A registered Asset is not necessarily monitored, connected, healthy, or
protected.

HUMAN DECISION BOUNDARY

Alert
→ Investigation
→ Human Verification
→ Deterministic State Evaluation
→ Human Closure When Eligible

Verification and closure remain separate decisions.

A confirmed-expected result may become eligible for closure only after the
required evidence conditions are satisfied. It does not close automatically.

Confirmed-unexpected activity remains under investigation.

Unable-to-verify and insufficient-information results preserve uncertainty.

No result automatically proves that an event is safe or malicious.
