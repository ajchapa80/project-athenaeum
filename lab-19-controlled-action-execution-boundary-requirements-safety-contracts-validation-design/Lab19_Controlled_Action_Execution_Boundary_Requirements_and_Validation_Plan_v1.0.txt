REQUIREMENTS, SAFETY CONTRACTS, AND VALIDATION DESIGN

======================================================================
1. PURPOSE
======================================================================

Lab 19 defines the vendor-neutral contract between an existing
READY_FOR_ACTION eligibility result and any future controlled action.

It answers:

What must be proven before an eligible, approved security response may
execute, and what evidence is required before any condition may be marked
resolved?

This is a requirements and design lab with tabletop acceptance cases. It does
not implement or operate an execution subsystem.

5. FROZEN ARCHITECTURAL RULES
======================================================================

R1. READY_FOR_ACTION is eligibility only.

R2. An executor may not decide whether an action is authorized.

R3. Missing, stale, mismatched, denied, or unverifiable approval fails closed.

R4. Approval must bind to the intended action and target.

R5. The target must be explicit, unique, in scope, and validated before
execution.

R6. Unsupported actions fail closed.

R7. Duplicate requests must not cause duplicate execution.

R8. A repeated execution attempt requires an explicit permitted retry state;
it may not be inferred from a prior failure or timeout.

R9. Command submission does not equal success.

R10. Execution success does not equal verification.

R11. Verification must be independent of the executor's success response
wherever technically practical.

R12. Verification failure, unavailability, or inconclusive evidence prohibits
resolution.

R13. Reversible actions require a defined rollback trigger, method, and
verification expectation before execution.

R14. Rollback completion must itself be independently verified.

R15. No condition may become resolved solely because an action returned
success.

R16. Prior AR, TR, PD, and AP records remain immutable historical decisions.
Future execution records reference them; they do not replace them.

R17. Every validation, execution, verification, rollback, and outcome state
must remain visible in the audit chain.

R18. Unknown or contradictory evidence fails closed and remains visible.

R19. Successful rollback proves that the attempted change was reversed; it
does not prove that the original security condition was resolved.

R20. Outcome/audit recording must preserve the complete chronological
execution history. It may not overwrite or hide earlier action requests,
execution attempts, verification failures, rollback activity, or prior
decisions.

6. PUBLIC-SAFE RECORD RESPONSIBILITIES
======================================================================

The following definitions describe responsibilities only. They are not
proprietary production schemas.

6.1 ACTION REQUEST RECORD (AQR)

Purpose:
Bind one requested action to the existing AR/TR/PD/AP decision chain.

Responsibilities:
- unique action-request identity;
- references to the relevant AR, TR, PD, and AP identities;
- requested action category and version;
- intended target identity and target type;
- scope or tenant context when applicable;
- request creation and expiration timestamps;
- approval identity, decision, scope, and validity interval;
- expected result and independent verification requirement;
- reversibility classification and rollback requirement;
- request status and validation warnings;
- no embedded secrets or executable credentials.

6.2 EXECUTION RECORD (EXR)

Purpose:
Record executor-side validation and any permitted execution attempt without
granting authorization.

Responsibilities:
- unique execution identity;
- AQR reference and immutable correlation identifier;
- executor identity and supported-action version;
- validation results for approval, target, support, and duplicate checks;
- idempotency key and prior-attempt disposition;
- attempt number and permitted retry basis;
- start, submission, completion, and timeout timestamps;
- execution state and bounded result summary;
- partial-execution indicator;
- verification-required indicator;
- no claim that execution success proves the intended condition.

6.3 VERIFICATION RECORD (VR)

Purpose:
Record an independent observation of the intended post-action condition.

Responsibilities:
- unique verification identity;
- references to AQR and EXR;
- verification method, verifier identity, and independence statement;
- expected condition and observed condition;
- evidence references and observation timestamps;
- succeeded, failed, unavailable, or inconclusive result;
- confidence limitations and missing evidence;
- rollback recommendation when applicable;
- resolution-eligibility effect.

6.4 ROLLBACK RECORD (RBR)

Purpose:
Record a required rollback and its independently verified outcome.

Responsibilities:
- unique rollback identity;
- references to AQR, EXR, and triggering VR;
- rollback trigger and authorization basis;
- defined restoration target;
- rollback attempt identity and timestamps;
- rollback execution result;
- independent rollback-verification method and evidence references;
- verified, failed, unavailable, or inconclusive restoration result;
- escalation and unresolved-state requirement.

6.5 OUTCOME/AUDIT RECORD (OAR)

Purpose:
Provide a complete, immutable summary of the decision-to-outcome chain and
determine whether resolution is eligible.

Responsibilities:
- unique outcome identity;
- references to AR, TR, PD, AP, AQR, EXR, VR, and RBR when present;
- ordered state-transition history and timestamps;
- validation failures and blocked-transition reasons;
- execution, verification, and rollback outcomes kept distinct;
- final resolution-eligibility decision and supporting verification reference;
- explicit unresolved reason when positive verification is absent;
- evidence-integrity and audit-completeness indicators;
- sanitization boundary for public reporting.

History behavior:
- preserve each AQR, EXR, VR, optional RBR, and its relationships;
- preserve chronological ordering and the original state of each event;
- append or reference later outcomes without replacing earlier history;
- retain blocked, failed, partial, inconclusive, and rollback states;
- represent an execution failure followed by successful rollback as two
  distinct facts, not as a generic successful or completed outcome.

6.6 TRACEABILITY CHAIN

AR -> TR -> PD -> AP -> AQR -> EXR -> VR -> optional RBR -> OAR

Each record has its own identity and lifecycle. A future record may reference
an earlier record but may not overwrite, reinterpret, or silently replace it.

7. DETERMINISTIC STATE MODEL
======================================================================

READY_FOR_ACTION
  Meaning: The existing Lab 17 upstream state indicates potential eligibility
  for execution validation.
  Allowed next state: EXECUTION_VALIDATION_PENDING.
  Prohibition: No command may execute from this state.
  Implementation boundary: Lab 19 consumes this conceptually but does not
  implement a consumer, executor, or action adapter.

EXECUTION_VALIDATION_PENDING
  Meaning: Approval, target, support, scope, expiry, and duplicate checks are
  pending or incomplete.
  Allowed next states: EXECUTING or NOT_RESOLUTION_ELIGIBLE.

EXECUTING
  Meaning: All pre-execution validations passed and one bounded attempt is in
  progress.
  Allowed next states: EXECUTION_FAILED or
  EXECUTION_COMPLETED_UNVERIFIED.

EXECUTION_FAILED
  Meaning: The action did not complete as intended, timed out, or returned an
  unacceptable result.
  Allowed next states: ROLLBACK_REQUIRED or NOT_RESOLUTION_ELIGIBLE.

EXECUTION_COMPLETED_UNVERIFIED
  Meaning: The executor reports completion, but the intended condition has not
  been independently verified.
  Allowed next states: VERIFICATION_SUCCEEDED, VERIFICATION_FAILED, or
  VERIFICATION_INCONCLUSIVE.

VERIFICATION_SUCCEEDED
  Meaning: Independent evidence positively confirms the intended condition.
  Allowed next state: RESOLUTION_ELIGIBLE.

VERIFICATION_FAILED
  Meaning: Independent evidence contradicts or does not confirm the intended
  condition.
  Allowed next states: ROLLBACK_REQUIRED or NOT_RESOLUTION_ELIGIBLE.

VERIFICATION_INCONCLUSIVE
  Meaning: Verification is unavailable, incomplete, stale, or ambiguous.
  Allowed next states: ROLLBACK_REQUIRED when required by policy, otherwise
  NOT_RESOLUTION_ELIGIBLE.

ROLLBACK_REQUIRED
  Meaning: The execution contract requires restoration because of failure,
  partial execution, adverse verification, or another frozen trigger.
  Allowed next state: ROLLBACK_EXECUTING.

ROLLBACK_EXECUTING
  Meaning: One authorized rollback attempt is in progress.
  Allowed next states: ROLLBACK_VERIFIED or ROLLBACK_FAILED.

ROLLBACK_VERIFIED
  Meaning: Independent evidence confirms restoration to the defined safe
  condition.
  Allowed next state: NOT_RESOLUTION_ELIGIBLE, followed by reassessment or
  human review as appropriate. Successful rollback proves reversal of the
  attempted change; it does not prove that the original security condition was
  resolved. A separate evidence chain would be required to evaluate that
  underlying condition.

ROLLBACK_FAILED
  Meaning: Restoration failed or could not be verified.
  Allowed next state: NOT_RESOLUTION_ELIGIBLE with escalation.

RESOLUTION_ELIGIBLE
  Meaning: Positive independent verification permits a separate resolution
  decision. This state does not itself mark the condition resolved.

NOT_RESOLUTION_ELIGIBLE
  Meaning: The evidence does not permit resolution. The condition remains
  unresolved and the reason remains auditable.

======================================================================

------------------------------  --------------------------------  ---------------------

10. TABLETOP ACCEPTANCE-CASE MATRIX
======================================================================

ID   Case                              Expected behavior / allowed transition
---- --------------------------------- ---------------------------------------
T01  Valid authorization               Continue to remaining validations only.
T02  Missing approval                  Fail closed; no execution.
T03  Stale approval                    Fail closed; require new approval.
T04  Denied approval                   Fail closed; no execution or retry.
T05  Approval for wrong target         Fail closed; no target substitution.
T06  Approval for wrong action         Fail closed; no action substitution.
T07  Ambiguous target                  Fail closed; require explicit identity.
T08  Nonexistent target                Fail closed; no execution.
T09  Unsupported action                Fail closed; no fallback action.
T10  Duplicate action request          Return prior/current disposition; do not
                                         execute twice.
T11  Repeated execution attempt        Block unless an explicit retry contract
                                         and current authorization permit it.
T12  Execution success                 Move only to
                                         EXECUTION_COMPLETED_UNVERIFIED.
T13  Execution failure                 Move to EXECUTION_FAILED; evaluate
                                         rollback; prohibit resolution.
T14  Partial execution                 Treat as failure/uncertain; require
                                         rollback assessment and verification.
T15  Verification success              Move to VERIFICATION_SUCCEEDED, then
                                         RESOLUTION_ELIGIBLE.
T16  Verification failure              Move to VERIFICATION_FAILED; prohibit
                                         resolution; roll back when required.
T17  Verification unavailable          Move to VERIFICATION_INCONCLUSIVE; keep
                                         unresolved.
T18  Rollback required                 Move only to ROLLBACK_EXECUTING after
                                         rollback authorization is validated.
T19  Rollback success response         Remain unverified until independent
                                         restoration evidence exists.
T20  Rollback verification failure     Move to ROLLBACK_FAILED; escalate and
                                         keep unresolved.
T21  Rollback failure                  Move to ROLLBACK_FAILED; preserve full
                                         evidence and escalate.
T22  Resolution attempted without VR   Reject transition; record prohibited
                                         attempt; keep unresolved.

Clarification for T19-T21:
Successful independent rollback verification moves through ROLLBACK_VERIFIED
to NOT_RESOLUTION_ELIGIBLE. It proves restoration of the attempted change, not
resolution of the original security condition. Reassessment or human review
is required as appropriate.

11. PUBLIC-SAFE THREAT AND SAFETY ANALYSIS
======================================================================

Threat / failure mode                 Required control
------------------------------------  ----------------------------------------
Eligibility confused with authority   Separate AQR validation from upstream
                                      READY_FOR_ACTION.
Executor self-authorizes              Executor consumes an external approval
                                      reference and may not create approval.
Expired approval reused               Freshness and validity interval check.
Action/target substitution            Exact approval binding and target match.
Duplicate delivery                    Stable idempotency key and prior-attempt
                                      lookup.
Timeout followed by blind retry       Treat outcome as uncertain and verify
                                      before any retry.
Unsupported action fallback           Fail closed; no nearest-action mapping.
False success from executor           Independent verification requirement.
Verification by same success signal   Separate verifier evidence and identity.
Partial execution hidden              Explicit partial state and rollback gate.
Rollback assumed successful           Independent rollback verification.
Premature resolution                  OAR requires positive VR reference.
Audit gaps                            Immutable ordered references and visible
                                      blocked-transition reasons. Later events
                                      append to history and never replace an
                                      earlier request, failure, verification,
                                      or rollback fact.
Evidence or log as instruction        Treat all collected content as untrusted
                                      evidence, never executable direction.

Residual uncertainty:

- Exact production time windows, target-authority sources, retry limits, and
  verifier implementations remain future private implementation decisions.
- Some actions may be non-reversible. They require stricter eligibility and
  are excluded from the first controlled implementation candidate.
- Independence may be constrained by a target platform. Any limitation must
  be explicit and may block resolution eligibility.

12. PASS / FAIL CRITERIA
======================================================================

DESIGN VALIDATION PASS requires all of the following:

- all T01-T22 cases have deterministic expected outcomes;
- unsafe cases fail closed;
- no executor grants its own authorization;
- approval is bound to action and target and checked for freshness;
- targets are explicit and validated;
- unsupported actions do not execute;
- duplicate requests cannot cause duplicate execution;
- command submission is distinct from execution completion;
- execution completion is distinct from verification;
- verification failure or unavailability prohibits resolution;
- reversible actions define rollback before execution;
- rollback completion requires independent verification;
- successful rollback leads to NOT_RESOLUTION_ELIGIBLE and does not prove the
  original security condition resolved;
- resolution eligibility requires positive independent verification;
- prior AR/TR/PD/AP records remain preserved and traceable;
- OAR preserves complete chronological history without collapsing or hiding
  earlier requests, attempts, failures, verification, or rollback activity;
- no public artifact exposes private implementation detail;
- no action is executed and no endpoint is modified during Lab 19.

FAIL applies if any unsafe case can reach EXECUTING or RESOLUTION_ELIGIBLE, if
record relationships are ambiguous, if duplicate execution is possible, if
success can bypass verification, if rollback can be assumed without evidence,
or if private implementation details enter public artifacts.

INCOMPLETE applies when a requirement cannot be evaluated without guessing.
The uncertainty must remain visible and the affected transition must remain
blocked.
